Event Marketer: why a low-tech trade show strategy gave AMEX OPEN an edge at CES

This year's CES gave us an opportunity to push our insight from past shows. The people on the floor are hungry, there's no one and nowhere to get a snack, and people are overwhelmed with technology. So we kept the activation simple: “Learn. Play. Eat.”

Customers got a chance to play our small-business game while we drove acquisition. Our business representatives singled out the small-business owners through the game we developed; they went on to win snacks from our small-business partners and a chance to win $500 in American Express gift cards. The event was a success and exceeded all client expectations.
