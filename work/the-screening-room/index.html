The Screening Room, a unique movie-theater restaurant on Canal Street, used to be the only operating theater south of Canal. Today the location houses the Tribeca Film Center.

I'd always wanted to collaborate on a project with my associate, so we approached the owners and proposed an ad for them on our own time. It ran in Paper magazine, the Village Voice, the New York Press and Time Out.
