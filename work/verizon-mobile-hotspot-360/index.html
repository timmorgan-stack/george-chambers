The 360 Display

Project

4G LTE Mobile Hotspot, in-store

After a few rounds of designs with existing vendors that failed, Verizon approached Hill Holliday/Erwin Penland to rethink the display for Verizon's 4G LTE Mobile Hotspot device.

From the range of concepts presented, Verizon selected my design for production. The 360 design allowed for placement in any type of store layout, regardless of size or pre-existing fixtures.

The top messaging rotates, and the devices on the platform below, with an interactive demonstration, reinforce the speed and power of the Mobile Hotspot. The display can be repurposed for other promotions. Tested in 25 stores throughout the tri-state area, it increased traffic in that area of the store and sales interaction with customers.
