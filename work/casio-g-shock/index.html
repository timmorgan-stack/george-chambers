SHOCK Magazine

Project

Print design

Client
Casio G-Shock
Agency
Saatchi & Saatchi
Discipline
Print & outdoor
Sector
Consumer

During my time at Saatchi & Saatchi, Casio asked for a design for their G-SHOCK quarterly brochure, for in-store and direct mail distribution. The brochure reflected the retro-yet-modern feel and versatility of the watch: rugged enough for snowboarding and ice hockey, stylish enough for music festivals.

Casio was very happy with the brochure and assigned the agency several more projects for G-Shock and other Casio products.
