Oddball

Project

Music video & design

Client
HEAP
Discipline

Sector
Consumer

Watch the film

I collaborated with director Graham Elliot on a music video, “Oddball”, for the band HEAP. Graham directed all the early Living Colour music videos and designed the album cover for the multi-platinum album “Vivid”.

Coney Island, the bright colors of the amusement park and all the oddballs that hang out there inspired the video. We took stills during the Mermaid Parade six months before the shoot to intersperse throughout. We shot in the winter, when the beaches are empty but the sun still shines. “Oddball” captures the spirit of the old Coney Island before the developers arrived. The video appeared on local cable stations, the HEAP website and YouTube.
