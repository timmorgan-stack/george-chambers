Re-Legalize

Project

Print, outdoor & digital

Client
NORML
Discipline
Print & outdoor, Digital & social
Sector
Pharma & health

I designed and wrote a campaign for NORML. Whether you are pro- or anti-marijuana, the tide is turning in the US. Various parts of the plant can be used to make textiles, paper, paints, clothing, plastics, cosmetics, foodstuff, insulation, animal feed and a myriad of other products to replace methods currently poisoning the earth.

Over 60 US and international health organizations support granting patients immediate legal access to medical marijuana. Over 30 nations, including Canada, Japan and the European Union, harvest the plant. You can also pump it into your biofuel gas tank, like Henry Ford did with his early Model T.

The campaign needed a timeless and serious tone, which would be unexpected: to educate skeptical consumers and prompt them to visit the website.
