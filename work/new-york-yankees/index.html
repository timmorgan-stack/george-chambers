Spring Training Getaway

Project

Event design

Quote
For someone who grew up playing baseball in NYC, they were the dream client.
Client
New York Yankees
Discipline
Experiential, Print & outdoor
Sector
Consumer

The New York Yankees and their sponsors chose a retro theme for the collateral for the sponsors' Spring Training getaway in Florida. The initial design eventually rolled out to event graphics and displays. Both the agency and the Yankees organization were very happy with the collateral, and for someone who grew up playing baseball in NYC, they were the dream client.
