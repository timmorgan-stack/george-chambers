Digital Sales Experience

Project

Store format & flow

Client
Verizon
Discipline
Retail & shopper, Digital & social
Sector
Consumer

To help customers engage with devices and navigate easily through stores, Verizon needed a new approach to store format and flow. The timing aligned with the launch of the new in-store branding.

ECD Lisa Diller and our team created an interactive sales experience by redesigning multiple aspects of the store and floor plan. The new design would surprise and delight the customer with technology instead of making them feel intimidated and overwhelmed.

Customers could begin their journey by online appointment, the My Verizon app or the in-store tablet. Check-in, now at the entrance, let customers interact with sales immediately. Clearly labeled sections, with headers above the line of sight, guide the customer through the store, and the accessories slide out in a drawer underneath each product.

The experience continued after the customer left the store, with follow-up messaging tailored to purchasing and non-purchasing customers. By triggering conversational selling with the tablet, the new design helps establish trust between the customer and the sales representative, and reinforces Verizon as a technology brand.
