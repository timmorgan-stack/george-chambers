(05)

This guy.

After graduating from FIT, I was hired straight away by advertising's dynamic duo, Charlie Moss and Stan Dragoti. Then New York, Dublin, New York again — and a career that runs from the shelf to the show floor to the doctor's office.

  1. Start
    FIT, then Moss/Dragoti. Hired straight out of school.
  2. Freelance
    Amster Yard. Sol Beer television.
  3. Dublin
    Art director. I followed my dream and moved to Ireland.
  4. New York
    Hill Holliday, Erwin Penland, Rauxa. Home to my family after 9/11, then a decade freelance and full-time on Verizon Wireless: digital, CRM, experiential, shopper marketing, TV, radio and music.
  5. Health
    FCB CURE, Biolumina, FCB NEON, Publicis Life Brands, Havas, Infranco/Moore, Saatchi & Saatchi LLKFB. Focusing my talents on pharma and health.
  6. Now
    Freelancing, interviewing and “writing that screenplay”. Clients of my own: The Shankill Market (Ireland), The Grand Hotel (England), Yoga Mountain (Ireland).
